# Forecast limitations for slides

- CMEMS and ERA5 data have operational latency; source-specific lag limits can force a variable to climatology.
- The training set covers approximately three years of aligned data.
- ERA5 atmospheric inputs use the 0.25° reanalysis grid.
- No demonstrated forecast skill is claimed for wind or rainfall; these remain climatology-backed in the production route.
- There is no automated storm detector; PAGASA advisories remain the final authority.
- The reported holdout evaluation covers April through September only and should not be generalized to all seasons.
